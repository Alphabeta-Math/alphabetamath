## External input

Under the standard analyticity, asymptotic-flatness and $I^+$-regularity hypotheses, a stationary vacuum black hole with at least one rotating horizon component is axisymmetric even when the horizon is disconnected. The stated geometric consequence of the balance result then forces the horizon to be connected, and the connected-horizon uniqueness result identifies its domain of outer communications with Kerr.

In a four-dimensional stationary, asymptotically flat, $I^+$-regular, analytic vacuum spacetime, no connected component of the future event horizon can be simultaneously non-rotating and degenerate. The horizon may be disconnected, and the statement applies even when other components rotate.

In a regular stationary axisymmetric smooth vacuum spacetime, for a degenerate horizon component, its area and angular momentum obey $A_i=8\pi|\mathcal J_i|$, with $A_i>0$ for a smooth nonempty cross-section. In the stationary asymptotically flat $I^+$-regular analytic vacuum class, if every horizon component is non-rotating and nondegenerate, the source's stated staticity/classification consequence is Schwarzschild and hence a connected horizon. Combining the rotating case and the Main Theorem, its final Corollary identifies the DOC with Kerr without imposing horizon connectedness.
