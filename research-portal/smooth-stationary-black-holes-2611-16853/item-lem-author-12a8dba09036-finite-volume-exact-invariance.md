## Statement

Let $C$ be a connected manifold with a Borel measure $\mu$ positive on every nonempty open set. Let $\psi_s$ be a complete flow by measure-preserving homeomorphisms. Let $B\subset C$ be closed, $\mu(B)<\infty$, $B=\overline{\operatorname{int}_CB}^{\,C}$, and $\psi_s(B)\subset\operatorname{int}_CB$ for every $s>0$. Then $\psi_s(B)=B$ for every $s>0$, and $B$ is open as well as closed. If $C\setminus B$ is nonempty, then $B=\varnothing$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] In its stated geometric setting, the inaccessible-set construction supplies the relative-interior and closure properties assumed here. [lem-author-12a8dba09036-inaccessible-set-relative-interior-density](item-lem-author-12a8dba09036-inaccessible-set-relative-interior-density)

[F2] Under the shift-flow lemma's hypotheses, its complete volume-preserving diffeomorphism flow and positive Riemannian Borel measure supply instances of the flow and measure data assumed here. [lem-author-12a8dba09036-complete-volume-preserving-shift-flow](item-lem-author-12a8dba09036-complete-volume-preserving-shift-flow)

[F3] The manifold $C$ carries its topology. Finite intersections of open sets are open, and a set is closed precisely when its complement is open. [math:def-topological-manifold-without-boundary](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-topological-manifold-without-boundary) [math:def-topological-space](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-topological-space)

[F4] The Borel sigma-algebra is generated by the open sets and is closed under complements and countable unions, hence also under differences. A Borel measure is a nonnegative, countably additive function on this sigma-algebra with $\mu(\varnothing)=0$. [math:def-borel-sigma-algebra](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-borel-sigma-algebra) [math:def-sigma-algebra](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-sigma-algebra) [math:def-measure](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-measure)

[F5] Here a complete flow by homeomorphisms means a continuous map $\psi:\mathbb R\times C\to C$, with $\psi_0=\operatorname{id}_C$ and $\psi_{s+t}=\psi_s\circ\psi_t$, each $\psi_s$ being a continuous bijection with continuous inverse $\psi_{-s}$. Measure preservation means $\mu(\psi_s^{-1}(E))=\mu(E)$ for every Borel set $E$, where $\psi_s^{-1}(E)$ denotes inverse image. [math:def-homeomorphism-and-open-maps](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-homeomorphism-and-open-maps) [math:def-measure-preserving-transformation-and-system](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-measure-preserving-transformation-and-system)

[F6] Interior is the largest open subset, and closure is the smallest closed superset, in the topology of $C$. A point belongs to the closure of a set exactly when every open neighborhood of that point meets the set. [math:def-interior-closure-boundary-top](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-interior-closure-boundary-top) [math:thm-closure-characterisation-top](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-closure-characterisation-top)

[F7] Connectedness means that there is no partition of $C$ into two nonempty disjoint open sets. [math:def-connected-space](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-connected-space)

## Proof

**Given:** The hypotheses in the Statement.

**Goal:** Establish exact invariance, openness and the stated empty-set conclusion.

1.1 Fix $s>0$ and put $A=\psi_s(B)$. Since $\psi_s$ is a homeomorphism, $A$ is closed: its complement is the inverse image of the open set $C\setminus B$ under the continuous inverse map $\psi_s^{-1}$. Thus $A$ and $B$ are Borel. Measure preservation applied to $A$ gives $\mu(A)=\mu(\psi_s^{-1}(A))=\mu(B)<\infty$, since bijectivity gives $\psi_s^{-1}(A)=B$. [given, F3, F4, F5]

2.1 The hypothesis gives $A\subset\operatorname{int}_CB\subset B$. The difference $B\setminus A$ is Borel. Apply countable additivity to the disjoint sets $A$, $B\setminus A$, followed by empty sets: $\mu(B)=\mu(A)+\mu(B\setminus A)$. The equality of the first two finite measures from step 1.1 yields $\mu(B\setminus A)=0$. [given, F4, F6, step 1.1]

3.1 Suppose $x\in B\setminus A$. The set $O=C\setminus A$ is open and contains $x$. Since $B=\overline{\operatorname{int}_CB}^{\,C}$, the closure characterization gives $V=O\cap\operatorname{int}_CB\ne\varnothing$. The set $V$ is open, so $\mu(V)>0$, and $V\subset B\setminus A$. Both $V$ and $(B\setminus A)\setminus V$ are Borel; finite additivity gives $0=\mu(B\setminus A)=\mu(V)+\mu((B\setminus A)\setminus V)\ge\mu(V)>0$, a contradiction. Therefore $\psi_s(B)=B$, as an exact set identity, for every $s>0$. [given, F3, F4, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 Fix any $s>0$. Equality from step 3.1 and the assumed forward inclusion give $B=\psi_s(B)\subset\operatorname{int}_CB\subset B$, so $B$ is open as well as closed. If $C\setminus B$ is nonempty and $B$ were nonempty, the two complementary open sets $B$ and $C\setminus B$ would be a separation of $C$. Connectedness rules this out, so $B=\varnothing$. [given, F3, F6, F7, step 3.1] ∎
