## Statement

Work in ZFC, including the Axiom of Choice and hence countable choice. Let $(M,g,N,o)$ be a smooth oriented, time-oriented Lorentzian four-manifold of signature $(-,+,+,+)$, with Levi–Civita connection $\nabla$. Let $T$ be a smooth Killing field, $m>0$ a constant, $\widehat g=m^{-2}g$ and $\widehat T=mT$. Keep the smooth manifold, orientation $o$ and time-orientation field $N$ fixed. The metric connections coincide and $\operatorname{Ric}(\widehat g)=\operatorname{Ric}(g)$; in particular, $\operatorname{Ric}(g)=0$ if and only if $\operatorname{Ric}(\widehat g)=0$.

For every fixed open domain $D\subset M$ and subset $U\subset D$, causal and timelike curves in $D$, their future and past directions, and their inextendibility are unchanged. Consequently the chronological sets $I_D^\pm(U)$, causal sets $J_D^\pm(U)$, the chronological exterior $I_D^-(U)\cap I_D^+(U)$, and the Cauchy sets in $D$ are unchanged. All smooth Killing fields for $g$ are exactly those for $\widehat g$. If $\phi^T$ is a local flow of $T$, then $\phi^{\widehat T}_s=\phi^T_{ms}$ on the rescaled flow domain. Their maximal orbit images coincide, and $T$ is complete if and only if $\widehat T$ is complete; no completeness is assumed.

If bifurcation data are supplied, fix a neighborhood $O$, two specified smooth embedded null hypersurfaces $H_+,H_-$ in $O$, and a smooth embedded spacelike two-surface $S$ diffeomorphic to the two-sphere. Require $H_+\cap H_-=S$ in $O$ and $T_pH_++T_pH_-=T_pM$ for $p\in S$; nullness means a one-dimensional radical of the induced form on each branch. Then the same branches and sphere satisfy these conditions for $\widehat g$. If the supplied branches are also achronal local null boundaries of the causal future and past of $S$, generated by null geodesics orthogonal to $S$, those conditions of regular bifurcate null horizon data are preserved too. No Killing generator is assumed to exist.

On any fixed smooth spacelike section $\Sigma$, let $n$ be its future unit normal, $h=g|_{T\Sigma}$ and $k(U,V)=-g(\nabla_U n,V)$ for tangent $U,V$. Then $\widehat n=mn$, $\widehat h=m^{-2}h$ and $\widehat k=m^{-1}k$ as covariant tensors, and $\operatorname{tr}_{\widehat h}\widehat k=m\operatorname{tr}_h k$; hence maximality is preserved. On two-forms, $\star_{\widehat g}=\star_g$. This is a constant homothety of the same section, not a replacement by an end-normalized graph.

## Proof

**Given:** The complete data and conditional hypotheses in the Statement, with the inherited choice assumptions recorded in A1.

**Goal:** Establish every stated preservation and scaling claim for the same original section.

1.1 In the Christoffel formula, $\widehat g^{-1}=m^2g^{-1}$ and $\partial\widehat g=m^{-2}\partial g$ have cancelling factors because $m$ is constant. Thus $\widehat\nabla=\nabla$. The curvature operator is built from this connection alone, and its Ricci tensor is the trace of $z\mapsto R(z,w)v$, so both are unchanged. This proves the Ricci equality and the stated equivalence of Ricci-flatness. [given, A1, F1, algebra]

1.2 For any smooth field $X$ with local flow $\phi^X$, differentiating $(\phi^X_s)^*\widehat g=m^{-2}(\phi^X_s)^*g$ at zero gives $\mathcal L_X\widehat g=m^{-2}\mathcal L_Xg$. Thus $X$ is Killing for one metric exactly when it is Killing for the other. The map $\widehat\phi(s,p)=\phi^T(ms,p)$ on $\{(s,p):(ms,p)\in\operatorname{dom}\phi^T\}$ is smooth, starts at $p$, has derivative $mT$ and satisfies the flow law. Replacing $m$ by $m^{-1}$ reverses this correspondence. [given, A1, F3, algebra]

2.1 For every tangent vector $v$, $\widehat g(v,v)=m^{-2}g(v,v)$ and $\widehat g(v,N)=m^{-2}g(v,N)$. Both signs are unchanged. The chart regularity and almost-everywhere nonzero-velocity conditions are metric independent, so exactly the same parametrized causal and timelike curves, with the same directions, are allowed in each fixed $D$. Endpoint limits use the unchanged topology, so inextendibility is identical. The reachability sets are therefore identical, and each Cauchy-set predicate counts the same parameter values over exactly the same inextendible curves. [F1, F2, step 1.1]

2.2 If $\gamma:I\to M$ is a maximal integral curve of $T$, then $s\mapsto\gamma(ms)$ on $m^{-1}I$ is an integral curve of $mT$. Any extension would, by the inverse reparameterization, extend $\gamma$, so it is maximal as well. Parameter multiplication by $m$ is a bijection of these intervals and of $\mathbb R$; hence the orbit images agree and every maximal interval is $\mathbb R$ for one field exactly when it is for the other. This proves the conditional completeness claim. [F3, step 1.2]

2.3 The vector $mn$ is future-directed, is orthogonal to $T\Sigma$, and satisfies $\widehat g(mn,mn)=-1$, so it is the hatted future unit normal. Restriction gives $\widehat h=m^{-2}h$. With the common connection and constant $m$, $\widehat k(U,V)=-m^{-2}g(\nabla_U(mn),V)=m^{-1}k(U,V)$. Contracting with $\widehat h^{-1}=m^2h^{-1}$ gives $\operatorname{tr}_{\widehat h}\widehat k=m\operatorname{tr}_h k$, which preserves zero trace on this same section. [A1, F1, F5, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 For supplied bifurcation data, the induced forms on $TH_\pm$ are multiplied by $m^{-2}$, so their radicals and nullness are unchanged. Spacelikeness of $S$ is preserved by the positive factor; the branch intersections, transversality and sphere topology use the unchanged embeddings. If the additional regular-null-horizon conditions hold, step 2.1 preserves the local causal futures and pasts of $S$ and achronality in $O$. Their boundaries are unchanged because the topology is fixed. The common connection preserves the generating null geodesics, and $\widehat g(L,V)=m^{-2}g(L,V)$ preserves their orthogonality to $S$. Thus the same pair and sphere retain all the specified conditions, without invoking any Killing generator. [given, A1, F1, F2, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 In four dimensions the oriented volume form scales by $\sqrt{|\det(m^{-2}g)|}/\sqrt{|\det g|}=m^{-4}$. The inverse-metric pairing on two-forms scales by $m^4$. Therefore for all two-forms $\alpha,\beta$ the defining identity gives $\alpha\wedge\star_{\widehat g}\beta=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle_g\,\mathrm{vol}_g=\alpha\wedge\star_g\beta$. Uniqueness of the Hodge map in that identity proves $\star_{\widehat g}=\star_g$, completing all claims. [A1, F6, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
