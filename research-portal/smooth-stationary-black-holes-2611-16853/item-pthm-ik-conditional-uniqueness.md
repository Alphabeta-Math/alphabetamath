## Statement

Work in ZFC in the regular smooth stationary vacuum model, and let $U_0$ be its designated physical stationary end, $E=I^-_M(U_0)\cap I^+_M(U_0)$, $C=\Sigma_0\cap E$ and $S$ its bifurcation sphere. Assume $T|_S\not\equiv0$ and that the actual physical exterior section $C$ is maximal, meaning its mean curvature is zero on $C$. Maximality of a separately specified coordinate side is insufficient unless that side is $C$; no agreement of the independent global section chart and physical end chart is assumed. Supply the same smooth Ernst potential $\sigma$ on an open neighborhood of $\Sigma_0\cup E$, with $d\sigma=2\iota_T\mathcal F$ and angularly uniform $\sigma\to1$ on the original physical $t=0$ end, in the declared Killing, curvature and orientation conventions. Assume the horizon conditions $-4M^2\mathcal F^2=(1-\sigma)^4$ on all of $S$ and $\operatorname{Re}(1/(1-\sigma))>1/2$ at some point of $S$, where $M$ is the coefficient in the original asymptotic expansion.

Then $M>0$, and $E$ is locally isometric to a Kerr domain of outer communication of mass $M$ with $0<a_{\rm loc}<M$, exactly in the sense of the IK local criterion. Separately, its reciprocal-invariant supplier gives $1-\sigma\ne0$ and $\mathcal S=0$ throughout $E$ and a single constant $B=z^2+4M^2(y^2+z^2)g^{-1}(dz,dz)$, with $y+iz=(1-\sigma)^{-1}$ and $0<B<1/4$. The normalized stationary end has physical ADM angular momentum magnitude $J$, with $B=J^2/(4M^4)$ and $0<J<M^2$.

For the working-spacetime construction, additionally assume two-sided chronological access to the sufficiently far selected normalized stationary end $U_f\subset U_0\cap E$: $I^-_E(U_f)=I^+_E(U_f)=E$, with all access curves contained in $E$. The selected end is beyond the end-normalization and coordinate/time-cutoff thresholds. Access is required for the selected tail after all such choices, and is not inferred from equality of ambient DOCs. Then the construction below gives an open working spacetime $M'=E\cup O\subset M$, a partial Cauchy section $\Sigma'$ extending the cutoff section $C'$, and a global section chart agreeing with the normalized end chart. It retains the separate identities $\Sigma'\cap I^-_{M'}(U_f)=\Sigma'\cap I^+_{M'}(U_f)=C'$ and $O\cap\partial_{M'}I^\pm_{M'}(U_f)=O\cap\partial_M I^\pm_M(U_0)$, with the signs matched separately. The full double boundary in $M'$ is exactly $S$ and the DOC of $U_f$ in $M'$ is $E$.

The global Kerr conclusion has an additional, explicit source-matching hypothesis: the constructed tuple $(M',g|_{M'},T,\Sigma',U_f,\sigma)$ satisfies the complete source-native AIK GR/SBS/PK contract. This means a smooth connected oriented time-oriented paracompact vacuum four-manifold without boundary; an embedded partial Cauchy section with global chart $\{|x'|>1/2\}$ putting $S$ at radius one and extending to the end with $T=\partial_{t'}$; the angularly uniform differentiated end bounds $g_{00}=-1+2M/r'+O_6(r'^{-2})$, $g_{ij}=\delta_{ij}+O_6(r'^{-1})$ and $g_{0i}=-2\epsilon_{ijk}S^j x'^k/r'^3+O_6(r'^{-3})$, where $r'=|x'|$, $M>0$ and $J=|\mathbf S|<M^2$; global hyperbolicity of its DOC; both separate chronological-section equalities above; and nonvanishing of $T$ in $E$, with every stationary orbit complete and meeting the section. It also retains the actual smooth transverse null nonexpanding horizon boundaries near $S$, tangency of $T$ to both and $T|_S\not\equiv0$, the source-normalized Ernst and Mars–Simon fields on an open neighborhood of $\Sigma'\cap E$, and every regularity/neighborhood bound entering the finite constant $A$ in source (2.1)–(2.5). PK requires $|(1-\sigma)\mathcal S(T,E_\alpha,E_\beta,E_\gamma)|\le\varepsilon$ in its section-orthonormal frames for the sufficiently small positive source threshold depending on $A$. Under this whole matched hypothesis package, $E$ is globally isometric onto the full subextremal Kerr exterior of mass $M$ and physical ADM angular momentum magnitude $J=M|a|$, with $0<|a|<M$. The derived zero tensor satisfies PK, but does not replace any GR, SBS or regularity clause. No Kerr isometry is assumed as a hypothesis.

## Proof

**Given:** The full hypotheses in the Statement.

**Goal:** Establish the local IK and scalar/spin conclusions, the working-horizon identities under the explicit access hypothesis, and the global conclusion conditional on the fully matched AIK source package.

1.1 Apply [F1] using the explicit maximality hypothesis on the actual exterior section $C=\Sigma_0\cap E$. The conditional lapse/product conclusion gives positive stationary lapse, its equation and zero boundary value, and a complete Killing-flow product. Its intrinsic closure is smooth, complete and spherical; no agreement of an auxiliary global radial chart with the physical end is asserted. On the original physical end, the lapse expansion in the mass-flux lemma gives $N=1-M/r+O_1(r^{-2})$. The compact-core and physical-end conclusions of [F6] verify its exterior hypothesis, so that lemma gives $M>0$. [A1, F1, F3, F6]

1.2 By [F6] choose an adapted topological identification $j:\Sigma_0\to\{|z|>1/2\}$ with $j(S)=\{|z|=1\}$ and $j(C)=\{|z|>1\}$. It need not agree with the physical end chart $x$. Choose a smooth nonincreasing function $\chi$ equal to one near $1/2$ and zero for $u\ge3/4$. For example, normalize the integral from $u$ to $3/4$ of the positive bump $\exp(-1/(v-5/8)-1/(3/4-v))$ on $5/8<v<3/4$, extended by zero, and extend the resulting cutoff constantly at either end. The function $a(u)=u-\chi(u)/2$ has $a'\ge1$, endpoint limits zero and infinity, and equals $u$ for $u\ge3/4$. Thus $z=a(|j|)j/|j|$ is a diffeomorphism from $\Sigma_0$ to punctured $\mathbb R^3$, unchanged near $S$ and on $C$. Add the point $p_0$ at $z=0$ to obtain an auxiliary smooth manifold $W\cong\mathbb R^3$, and let $D_0=\{|z|\le1\}$. This is a parametrized smooth closed ball with center $p_0$ and boundary $S$. This auxiliary filling is only a spatial-coordinate construction; it adds no point to the spacetime or its metric. [A1, F6, construct, algebra]

2.1 Choose a sufficiently large physical radius $R>2$ so that the compact physical truncation of [F6] is defined and a two-sided coordinate collar of $\Gamma_R=\{t=0,|x|=R\}$ lies in the physical end.
