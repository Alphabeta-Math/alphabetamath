## 1. Research insights beyond the article

### Research insight 01 · Area-charge deficit controls flux variance

#### Insight

**Kind:** corollary

**Evidence status:** proved

Let $(S,\mathcal A,\mu)$ be a measure space with $A=\mu(S)\in(0,\infty)$, and let $e,b$ be real measurable functions with $E=\int_S(e^2+b^2)\,d\mu<\infty$. Set $Q=\int_Se\,d\mu$ and $V=\int_S((e-Q/A)^2+b^2)\,d\mu$. Then $E=Q^2/A+V$. If $E\le4\pi$, the deficit decomposes exactly as $4\pi-Q^2/A=V+(4\pi-E)$, so $0\le V\le4\pi-Q^2/A$ and $A\ge Q^2/(4\pi)$. In particular, if $q\in[0,1]$, $Q=4\pi\sqrt{1-q^2}$, $\epsilon\ge0$ and $A\le4\pi+\epsilon$, then $V\le4\pi(\epsilon+4\pi q^2)/(4\pi+\epsilon)$. These upper bounds on $V$ are sharp over finite measure spaces. For every $t>0$, $\mu(\{(e-Q/A)^2+b^2>t^2\})\le V/t^2$. If the energy assumption is relaxed to $E\le4\pi+\eta$, with $\eta\ge0$, both upper bounds on $V$ acquire the additive term $\eta$. Zero deficit under the exact energy assumption forces $e=Q/A$ and $b=0$ almost everywhere.

#### Why it matters

This quantifies the square-completion mechanism used in the article's horizon saturation argument. It separates flux variation from unused energy budget and permits approximate integral data. Geometric context is conditional: for a connected closed orientable spacelike MOTS, spacetime outer stability, Einstein's equations with standard Maxwell stress, nonnegative cosmological constant and dominant-energy non-electromagnetic matter give the required energy bound. This is a conditional physics-theorem, with physical assumptions rather than measurements; it is contextual and supplies no premise to the abstract proof. See [Dain–Jaramillo–Reiris, §3, (31)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1109.5602). Here $Q$ denotes unnormalized flux.
