## Postulate

In the stationary vacuum model, restrict this motivation to smooth four-dimensional spacetimes with a selected asymptotically flat end and $I^+$-regularity in the comparison-data convention. Stationarity means that the specified Killing field $T$ is future timelike sufficiently far out on that end. For its flow-saturated exterior $M_{\rm ext}$ and domain of outer communications $D$, count components of the specific future horizon portion $\mathcal E^+=\partial_M D\cap I_M^+(M_{\rm ext})$. Here multiple black holes means that this end-relative portion has several connected components.

As qualitative physical intuition, gravitational interaction between black holes is expected to be attractive. This motivates the expectation against regular stationary multiple-black-hole configurations, described in the article as a longstanding conjecture. In this item the expectation has the vacuum and regularity scope just specified; it is not an unrestricted assertion for arbitrary matter models.

Attraction alone does not decide balance: spin-spin or electrostatic repulsion may oppose it. In particular, static equilibria of several extremal charged black holes are known in Einstein-Maxwell theory, outside the vacuum scope here. This postulate records motivation, not a force law, observation, proof of nonexistence or premise establishing the conjecture. No analyticity or nondegeneracy restriction is imposed on the motivation.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The comparison-data definition supplies stationarity relative to the selected asymptotically flat end, $M_{\rm ext}$, $D$, $\mathcal E^+$ and $I^+$-regularity. Its regularity conditions are imposed formulation data, not consequences of attraction. [[def-black-hole-comparison-hypothesis-data]]

[F2] The stationary vacuum model supplies the adopted vacuum equation $\operatorname{Ric}(g)=0$ and stationary-field conventions. It fixes the context of the expectation and does not derive it. [[post-stationary-vacuum-model]]

[F3] Hennig's independently consulted introduction supplies the qualitative balance motivation and the charged-equilibrium caveat. Neither is used to deduce a general vacuum nonexistence result.

## Source notes

The studied article is cited solely to attribute its intuitive attraction claim and conjectural motivation. Its later analytic-vacuum discussion is separate and is not used to establish the expectation.

Hennig's technical scope is aligned axisymmetric configurations, with a focus on subextremal holes. The introductory motivation is used qualitatively here; no extension of a restricted theorem to arbitrary smooth vacuum configurations is claimed.
