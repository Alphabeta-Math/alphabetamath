## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, that every family of nonempty sets has a choice function. Let $m$ be a positive odd integer, so $m=2a+1>0$ for an integer $a$. Let $p\in\mathcal P_3$, the primes of the form $4b+3$ for an integer $b$, and assume $p\nmid m$, meaning that $m$ is not an integer multiple of $p$. A prime is an integer greater than one with only itself and one as positive divisors.

Let $\mathcal H$ be the Hurwitz order, the subring of rational Hamilton quaternions with four coordinates either all integral or all in $\mathbb Z+\tfrac12$. These are coordinates in the basis $1,i,j,k$ with $i^2=j^2=-1$ and $ij=k=-ji$. The reduced norm $N(\alpha)$ is the sum of the squares of the four coordinates. The norm shell is $Q_m=\{\alpha\in\mathcal H:N(\alpha)=m\}$, and $\mathcal H^\times$ denotes the elements with a two-sided inverse in $\mathcal H$.

Let $G_p=\mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_p)$ consist of invertible two-by-two matrices over the field $\mathbb F_p$ of integer residues modulo $p$, with matrices identified up to nonzero scalar multiples. The fixed projective reduction $\rho_p$ takes $\alpha$ with $p\nmid N(\alpha)$ to the projective class of its image under the chosen matrix identification of $\mathcal H/p\mathcal H$. Put $\Gamma_p=\rho_p(\mathcal H^\times)$ and let $Y_p=\{\Gamma_p g:g\in G_p\}$, where the right coset $\Gamma_p g$ consists of the elements $\gamma g$ for $\gamma\in\Gamma_p$. The set $Y_p$ is finite since there are only finitely many matrices over $\mathbb F_p$, and it contains the identity coset.

Write $\mathbb C[Y_p]$ for the complex functions on $Y_p$ with pointwise addition and scalar multiplication. The quaternionic Hecke operator is the linear map given by the finite shell sum
$$(T_m f)(\Gamma_p g)=\frac1{24}\sum_{\alpha\in Q_m} f(\Gamma_p\rho_p(\alpha)g).$$
Let $\mathbf1$ be the constant function of value one, and let the determinant character $\chi_p$ take $\Gamma_p[A]$ to $1$ for square determinant and $-1$ for nonsquare determinant in $\mathbb F_p^\times$. Here $[A]$ denotes a projective class, and the determinant for rows $(a,b),(c,d)$ is $ad-bc$.

Use the counting inner product $\langle f,h\rangle_p=\sum_{y\in Y_p} f(y)\overline{h(y)}$ and the norm $\|f\|_p=\sqrt{\langle f,f\rangle_p}$. Complex conjugation sends $a+bi$ to $a-bi$ for real $a,b$. Let $W=\{\mathbf1,\chi_p\}^{\perp}$ be the subspace of functions whose inner product with each of the two functions vanishes. The single-coset indicator functions form a finite basis of $\mathbb C[Y_p]$, so $W$ is finite-dimensional. For a linear map $D:W\to W$ with $W\ne0$, its operator norm is the attained maximum of $\|Df\|_p$ over $\|f\|_p=1$. On the zero space its value is zero.

Then $T_m$ preserves $W$ and $\|T_m|_W\|\le\tau(m)\sqrt m$. Here $\tau(m)$ counts the positive divisors of $m$, and $\sqrt m$ is the unique nonnegative square root of $m$ in the complete ordered field of real numbers.

[F5] The discrete decomposition gives $\mathscr L^L=\bigoplus_i M_i^L$ with finitely many nonzero terms, pairwise orthogonal for the integral pairing.
